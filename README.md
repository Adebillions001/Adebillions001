# Hey, I'm Adeola 👋🏽

### 🤖 AI Automation Engineer · 🌱 Growing into Data Engineering

I enjoy taking messy, repetitive processes and figuring out how to make them work better.

I build automations that connect tools, move information between systems, and reduce manual work. I'm also developing my data engineering skills because I want to understand the full journey of data — from collection and processing to storage and making it useful.

---

## ✨ A little about my journey

I started exploring AI automation in late 2025. What began as curiosity about making everyday tasks easier has grown into building workflows for real operational problems.

These days, I'm building on that experience with Python, SQL, and the foundations of data engineering. I like understanding what happens under the hood, not just getting something to run.
